# GM3506 Brushless motor

The GM3506 is a compact, high-speed brushless DC (BLDC) motor designed primarily for gimbal and camera stabilization applications. It features a flat, pancake-style form factor with a hollow shaft, making it ideal for tight integration in multi-axis systems. Known for its high KV rating (typically around 1100 KV), the GM3506 delivers fast response and smooth operation when paired with appropriate encoders and motor drivers. Its low cogging torque and lightweight design contribute to precise motion control, especially in dynamic environments where stability and responsiveness are critical.
