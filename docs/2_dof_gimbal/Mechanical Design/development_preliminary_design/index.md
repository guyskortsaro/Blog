# Development Preliminary Design

The preliminary development design served as the foundation for early testing and hardware integration. It was intentionally kept simple and open to allow easy access to all key components, such as the motors, sensors, and drivers. This layout enabled rapid prototyping, streamlined troubleshooting, and smooth implementation of control algorithms. By prioritizing hardware handling and modularity at this stage, we ensured a flexible platform for refining both the mechanical and electronic systems before moving to the final design.
